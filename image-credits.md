## Note on human subjects

Pexels photographs showing solar installation work in Kenya or East Africa were not available under the free licence at build time. Rather than show installers who do not reflect the communities we serve, this build uses equipment, rooftop and aerial photographs of solar installations, and keeps people out of the photography. Client names and portraits are intentionally omitted. When commissioned photography of the Nairobi crews becomes available, drop the files into `public/images/` and update the `image` fields in `src/data/solutions.ts` and `src/data/projects.ts`.
